# Onboarding

## First day
Your laptop and accounts are ready before you arrive. If something is missing, raise it with
IT rather than waiting for it to resolve itself.

## The first week
A buddy from outside your immediate team is assigned for the first month. Their job is
questions you would feel silly asking your manager, and they are expected to make time for
them.

## Probation
The probation period is six months, with a formal check-in at three. Probation is not a
quality gate you pass by shipping a lot; it is a mutual check that the role is the right
one.
