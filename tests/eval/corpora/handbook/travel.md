# Travel

## Booking flights
Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Longer flights may be booked in
premium economy without further approval. Business class requires sign-off from a director
and is expected to be rare.

## Hotels
Hotel stays above 200 euros per night need written approval before booking. Where a
conference has a negotiated rate above that figure, forward the conference booking page and
approval is automatic.

## Ground transport
Taxis are reimbursed for airport transfers and for any journey after 22:00. Otherwise use
public transport or a rail card, both of which are claimed in the same way as any other
expense.
