# Expenses

## Filing a claim
Receipts must be filed within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted after that
window need written approval from your manager and the finance lead, and may be refused.
Photographs of paper receipts are accepted as long as the total, the date and the merchant
are all legible.

## Limits
Meals while working away from your usual office are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day.
Alcohol is never reimbursed, including when it appears on an otherwise valid meal receipt;
split the bill or deduct the amount before claiming.

## Payment
Approved claims are paid with the following month's salary. Claims approved after the 20th
of a month roll into the month after. There is no facility for same-week payment, so do not
plan around one.
