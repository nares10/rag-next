# Equipment

## What you get
The company provides a laptop and one external monitor to every employee. A keyboard,
mouse and headset are available on request through the IT portal rather than as an expense
claim.

## Replacement cycle
Hardware is replaced on a three year cycle. Replacement before then needs a fault report;
machines that are merely slow are reimaged first and only replaced if that does not help.

## Loss and damage
Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT security the same day so the device can be wiped
remotely. Accidental damage is covered by the company. Deliberate damage or loss through
clear negligence may be recharged to the employee.
