# Remote work

## Core hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your own local time zone. Outside those hours you are free
to arrange your day as you like, provided meetings you have accepted are honoured.

## Meetings
Any meeting with a remote participant is a remote meeting: everyone joins from their own
machine rather than crowding one room camera. Recordings are kept for 30 days.

## Home office
A one-off stipend of 500 euros covers a desk and chair. It is claimed as an expense and does
not need separate approval. Internet bills are not reimbursed.
