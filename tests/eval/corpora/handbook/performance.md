# Performance

## Review cycle
Reviews run twice a year, in April and October. Written feedback is collected from three
colleagues you nominate and at least one your manager nominates.

## Promotion
Promotion is decided in the April cycle only. A promotion case is written by your manager and
read by two directors who have not worked with you directly, which is why concrete examples
matter more than adjectives.

## Pay
Salary changes take effect the month after the cycle closes. A promotion and a market
adjustment can land in the same cycle and are shown separately on your letter.
