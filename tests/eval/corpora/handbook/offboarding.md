# Offboarding

## Notice
The notice period is one month for the first two years of service and three months after
that. Notice is given in writing to your manager and to people operations.

## Equipment return
Laptops and monitors are returned on or before your last day using the prepaid courier label
people operations send you. Keep the tracking number until the return is confirmed.

## Access
Access to email, source control and the cloud console is revoked at the end of your last
working day, not at the end of the notice period. Export anything personal before then.
