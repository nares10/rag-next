# Leave

## Annual leave
Everyone gets 28 days of paid annual leave a year, excluding public holidays in your
country of employment. Leave is accrued monthly but may be taken in advance within the same
calendar year.

## Carrying days over
Up to five unused days carry into the next year and must be used before the end of March.
Days beyond five are forfeited; there is no option to be paid for them.

## Sick leave
Report sickness to your manager on the first morning you are unable to work. A doctor's note
is required from the fourth consecutive day. Sick days do not count against annual leave.
