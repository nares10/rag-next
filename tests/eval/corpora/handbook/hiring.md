# Hiring

## Referrals
A referral bonus of 2000 euros is paid once the person you referred passes probation. There
is no limit on the number of referrals, but the bonus is not paid for contractors or for
anyone who has worked here before.

## The loop
The interview loop is four conversations: a screen, two craft interviews and one on working
with others. Candidates see the full list before they start.

## Debriefs
Interviewers write their notes before the debrief, not during it, and a debrief is cancelled
if the notes are not in. This is to stop the loudest voice in the room setting the tone.
