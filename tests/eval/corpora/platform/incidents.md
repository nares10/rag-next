# Incidents

## Severity
Sev1 means customer-visible loss of a core flow. Sev2 is degraded but working. Sev3 is
internal only. The person who notices sets the severity and nobody is criticised for setting
it too high.

## Paging
Sev1 and Sev2 page the on-call engineer immediately. Sev3 waits for working hours. The
on-call rotation is weekly and handover happens on Wednesday morning, not Monday, so a
quiet weekend is not the handover test.

## Postmortems
Every Sev1 gets a written postmortem within five working days. Postmortems name systems and
decisions, never people, and the action items have owners and dates or they do not count.
